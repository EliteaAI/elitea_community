# Elitea Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Elitea community is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, safe, and constructive environment for everyone.

This repository exists so community members can report bugs, request features, ask questions, share feedback, discuss ideas, and help improve Elitea. We want these spaces to support open collaboration without harassment, hostility, discrimination, or abuse.

We expect all participants — including maintainers, contributors, users, partners, and observers — to engage in a professional, respectful, and constructive way.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions in the Elitea community repository, including:

- GitHub Issues
- GitHub Discussions
- issue comments
- discussion comments and replies
- linked community conversations managed by the Elitea team
- any public or private communication related to participation in this community space

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the Elitea project or community in public spaces.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that help create a positive community environment include:

- being respectful, courteous, and professional
- assuming good intent while still giving clear feedback
- focusing on facts, reproduction details, and user impact
- being constructive when proposing changes or disagreeing with others
- welcoming people with different levels of experience
- helping redirect questions to the right place, such as Issues, Discussions, documentation, or support channels
- giving actionable, specific feedback instead of dismissive criticism
- respecting community guidelines, templates, and moderator instructions
- protecting privacy and avoiding the sharing of confidential or sensitive information

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, bullying, or abusive conduct
- discriminatory language or behavior based on personal characteristics
- personal attacks, insults, trolling, flaming, or deliberately inflammatory comments
- hostile or repetitive arguing that prevents constructive discussion
- doxxing, threats, or encouraging harm toward others
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- impersonation, deception, or intentionally misleading others
- posting spam, off-topic promotion, or disruptive content
- repeatedly derailing discussions, feature requests, or bug reports
- publishing another person's private, confidential, or identifying information without permission
- posting secrets or sensitive data, including passwords, tokens, API keys, customer data, internal endpoints, or exploit details
- filing issues or comments in bad faith
- using AI-generated or copied content to flood, manipulate, or disrupt community processes
- retaliating against anyone for reporting a Code of Conduct concern

## Community-specific guidance

Because this repository is used for bugs, feature requests, and public discussions, please also follow these practical guidelines.

### When reporting bugs
- stay factual and specific
- include steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and actual behavior
- avoid blame-oriented language
- do not accuse individuals or teams without evidence

### When requesting features
- explain the problem you want to solve
- be open to discussion and alternative solutions
- understand that a feature request may be reviewed, deferred, or declined

### When participating in Discussions
- keep topics in the correct category when possible
- disagree with ideas respectfully
- avoid turning product disagreement into personal conflict
- do not dominate conversations or dismiss other users' use cases

### When sharing logs, screenshots, or examples
- redact secrets and personal data
- remove customer-sensitive or confidential material
- share only what is necessary to explain the issue or idea

## Maintainer responsibilities

Elitea maintainers and moderators are responsible for helping protect the health of the community.

They may:

- clarify or enforce community expectations
- edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this Code of Conduct or repository guidelines
- redirect conversations to the correct channel
- ask users to revise posts that contain sensitive or inappropriate content
- close issues or discussions that are abusive, off-topic, duplicative, or otherwise inappropriate
- temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary

Maintainers are expected to enforce this Code of Conduct fairly, consistently, and in good faith.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness harassment, abuse, discrimination, threats, privacy violations, or other unacceptable behavior, please report it to the Elitea community team.

**Code of Conduct contact:** `SupportAlita@epam.com`

When making a report, please include if possible:

- a link to the relevant issue, discussion, or comment
- screenshots or copied text, if relevant
- the usernames involved
- a brief description of what happened
- any safety or urgency concerns

Please do not use public issues or discussions to report Code of Conduct concerns.

All reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical. The Elitea team will make a good-faith effort to review and respond appropriately. We may not be able to share every detail of any investigation or outcome.

## Enforcement

If behavior violates this Code of Conduct, the Elitea team may take any action it considers appropriate, including:

- a private or public warning
- content removal
- conversation locking
- temporary suspension from participation
- permanent removal from community spaces

The response will depend on the severity, pattern, and impact of the behavior.

## Good-faith participation

Constructive disagreement, critical product feedback, and honest bug reports are welcome.

Not every disagreement is a conduct violation. However, how feedback is delivered matters. Strong opinions are acceptable; disrespectful behavior is not.

We want this repository to be a place where people can:

- report problems clearly
- propose ideas openly
- ask questions safely
- collaborate respectfully
- help improve Elitea together
